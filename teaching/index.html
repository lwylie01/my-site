The Usual Subjects

Measurement and metrics: building performance measures that hold up, and knowing when a composite score helps and when it misleads.

Data visualization: choosing the chart that matches your argument, and showing uncertainty instead of false precision.

Program evaluation: matching the evaluation to the question and the program’s stage, and knowing what must be in place before outcomes mean anything.

Data storytelling: turning findings into a story a specific audience can act on, with a narrator they trust and a call to action you can measure.
